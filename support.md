# Podsy.fm Support


Need help? Email **contact@findmyorbit.com** — we usually reply within two business
days. Please include your device model, iOS version and app version
(Settings → Version in the app).

## Getting music into the app

1. On the main menu, choose **Add Music**, then **Choose files** and **Continue**. You can also pick a folder or a ZIP, or send files from a computer with **Wi-Fi transfer**.
2. Pick songs or a ZIP from the Files app, iCloud Drive, or another cloud
   drive shown in the picker (Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive and others add
   themselves once their app is installed).
3. You can also share or **Open in…** an audio file or ZIP from Files, Mail
   or Safari downloads and choose Podsy.fm.

The app keeps its own copy, so songs play offline. Your originals are never
changed.

**Supported formats:** MP3, AAC/M4A, Apple Lossless (ALAC), FLAC, WAV, AIFF.
**Not supported:** copy-protected (DRM) downloads from streaming services.

Only import music you own or have the right to play.

## Using the wheel

- Slide around the ring to scroll.
- Press the centre to choose.
- **MENU** (top) goes back.
- Left/right skip to the previous/next song; bottom plays or pauses.

Settings → **Welcome Tour** shows this again.

## Common questions

**A file says "Not supported" or "Damaged".** It isn't a format the app can
read, or it is incomplete. Try exporting it again from its source.

**A cloud file won't import.** Open the cloud app and make sure the file is
downloaded to the device, then try again.

**Songs show as missing.** Settings → File Management lists them. Remove them, then
import the originals again.

**I bought Lifetime Pro but it isn't active.** Go online, then Settings → Lifetime Pro →
**Restore purchase**, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it
with. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, not to an app account.

**Refunds.** Apple handles refunds: reportaproblem.apple.com.

**How do I free up space?** Settings → File Management: remove songs one at a time, or tap Remove all music.
This deletes only the app's copy.
