# Podsy.fm Privacy Policy

_Effective: November 7, 2026._


Podsy.fm ("the app") is published by Orbit AI Technologies Inc. ("we"). This
policy explains what happens to information when you use the app.

## Summary

**We do not collect any personal data.** The app has no accounts, no sign-in,
no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and we run no servers. Everything
you do in the app stays on your device, unless you choose to export or share it.

## Information stored on your device

- **Music you import.** When you choose files (from the Files app, iCloud
  Drive or another file provider) or open them in the app, the app copies them
  into its own private storage on your device so they can play offline. Your
  original files are only read; they are never changed or deleted.
- **Library data.** Song details read from your files (title, artist, album,
  artwork), playlists, favourites and recent searches.
- **Settings.** Preferences such as haptics, reduced motion and pausing when
  headphones disconnect.

This information never leaves your device through the app. Your device's own
backups (iCloud or computer) include your library data, designs and settings,
according to your device settings. The app's copies of your music and any photos
you add are left out of those backups, so after restoring to a new device, add
your music again and your playlists and favourites reconnect. Delete individual songs in Settings → File Management, or delete the app to
remove everything it stored.

## Wi-Fi transfer

If you open Wi-Fi transfer, your phone runs a small server on your local network
so a computer on the same network can send music to the app. It runs only while
that screen is open and for a limited time, and it stops if the app leaves the
screen. A computer needs a one-time code or QR code, and you approve each
connection on your phone. It accepts audio and ZIP files only and never serves
your library. Nothing goes to the internet or to us. The connection on your
network isn't encrypted, so use a network you trust. iOS asks your permission for
local network access the first time.

## Scanning for music (Android)

If you tap Scan this device on Android, the app asks for permission to read audio
files, then imports the music the system lists. It doesn't look at anything else.

## Exports and photos

Images, reveal videos and theme files you export are created on your device and
go only where you send them. Photos you added to a design are left out of theme
files unless you choose to include them, and the app warns you before you share
something that shows them. Reveal videos have no sound.

## How long drafts and photos are kept

Designs you save, and the photos in them, stay on your device until you delete
them or the app.

If Studio closes unexpectedly (for example, the app is closed while you're
editing), your unsaved draft and the photos in it are kept on your device for 24
hours so you can pick up where you left off. After that they are deleted the next
time you open the app.

Photos you add to a design but don't save are deleted when you leave Studio, or
the next time you open the app if Studio didn't close normally. While a saved
design on your device can't be read, these photos are kept until you delete that
design in My Players, because it may be the one using them.

## Files from other apps

The app only reads the specific files and folders you pick or open in it. Apart
from the Android scan above, it does not scan your device, your photo library, or
other apps' data.

## Purchases

Lifetime Pro is an optional one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple
through the App Store, or by Google through Google Play. The store handles
payment and your account; we never receive your name, email, payment details
or store account information. The app receives only a confirmation from the
store that you own Lifetime Pro, which it keeps on your device. Apple's and
Google's handling of purchase data is covered by their own privacy policies.

## Third parties

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and
it does not share or sell data to anyone.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including
children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country).

## Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to
access, correct, export or delete on our side. All app data is under your
control on your device.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date. If
the app ever starts collecting data, we will say so here and in the App Store
privacy details before that version is released.
